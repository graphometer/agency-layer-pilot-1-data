### What a constant answer scores, under each weighting

A metric cannot be read as if its floor were 0.5 when its floor is 0.82. This is the reason the
verdict-pool weighting is labelled circular: its large stratum is the one where both PM19 judges
already said `qualifying`, which is exactly where a lenient judge looks best.

## Verdict

No candidate satisfies the four measurable criteria of the written standard. The seated primary `mistral-primary` reaches raw agreement 0.8105 and weighted agreement 0.8959 against a 0.90 bar, with a prose-channel condition gap of 0.170 against a 0.05 cap; it meets recall (1.000) and stability (0.9919). The seated secondary `minimax-m3` reaches weighted agreement 0.8917 and stability 0.9113 against a 0.95 bar. The superseded PM20 text quoted 0.896 for the primary (weighted, strict convention: reproduced here as 0.8959) beside 0.903 for the secondary (weighted, scored-only convention: reproduced here as 0.9033), which mixed two denominators in one comparison, and 0.934 for the unseated Kimi candidate, which no convention here reproduces (its closest figures are 0.9313 scored-only weighted pass 1 and 0.9489 pass 2). Those three figures are withdrawn and replaced by this table. The qualification gate is recorded as FAILED; whether the study proceeds with a disclosed departure or with the R39 timing-alone fallback is a design ruling, not a measurement, and is recorded in DECISIONS_V0.1.md.
