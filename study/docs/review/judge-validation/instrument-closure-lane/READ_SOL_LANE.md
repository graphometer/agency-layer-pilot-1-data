AMEND BEFORE RUNNING

The five qualification criteria are faithfully carried onto the condition-invariant frame, and I find no other stated qualification gate unmeasurable at the frozen sample sizes. Two operational defects and one role-disclosure overstatement require amendment:

1. **Quote:** “higher precision on negatives — frozen as 1 minus the false-positive rate.” **Defect:** `1 − FPR = TN/(TN+FP)` is specificity, not negative-class precision `TN/(TN+FN)`. This can change the tie-break winner and makes “R43, verbatim” inaccurate. **Severity:** outcome-determinative if multiple judges qualify. **Minimal amendment:** either compute negative-class precision as `TN/(TN+FN)`, with a frozen zero-denominator rule, or explicitly amend R43 before scoring to say that its tie-break is specificity and use that term throughout.

2. **Quote:** “unanimous labels auto-accepted subject to a 20-item audit; if the audit disagrees with more than 1 of its 20 unanimous items...” **Defect:** no random-selection algorithm/seed is frozen, and it is unclear whether the 20 and the trigger apply jointly or separately to L1 and L2. The audit can therefore be selected after labels are visible and its escalation rule is not uniquely computable. **Severity:** material gold-set selection bias. **Minimal amendment:** before labelling, freeze an ID-hash sampling rule and define separate 20-decision audits and >1/20 escalation triggers for L1 and L2 (with their packets kept separate).

3. **Quote:** “Sol adjudicates nothing Sol built, Sol authors no gold-construction tooling.” **Defect:** Sol is also assigned `build_frame.py`; that projection supplies eligibility/content for the real gold strata, so the categorical disclosure is false even though Sol does not select items or generate near misses. **Severity:** conflict-disclosure defect, not a reason to replace the adjudicator. **Minimal amendment:** say that Sol authors the frozen, cross-reviewed frame projection but authors neither gold selection/assembly nor the near-miss generator.
